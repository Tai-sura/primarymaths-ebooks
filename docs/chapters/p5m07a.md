## Source facts

## How the answers show

Decision from `tools/layout_triage.py` (Stage 2a), taken before the solutions were written:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Mode | `answerCards` — fill-in questions open a card from a `?` chip; MC and long questions stay inline |
| Why | 18 of 59 questions have no full-width blank row to cut at (q4–q5 sit beside the big figure, q18–q22 inside the table, q26–q31 and hw1–hw3/hw8–hw10 in packed scan rows). Cutting there tears the figure or the table, and a page cut at a bad row put the answer card on the next question's text in the first build. |
| `answerCards` | the 46 fill-in questions: q1–q33 (except none), hw1–hw14 |
| Inline, physics style | MC q34–q41 (square/letter), long questions q42–q46 — region + page cut + `space` |
| `space` overrides | kept for q34–q40 (81/101/113/283/65/99/176 pt); q41–q46 use their roomy page bands |

The `?` chip sits at the top-right of the line that carries the blank (`layout.json` `cardAt`, from the row with the underscores), so a blank on a question's second line (q1, q4) gets its chip on that line. Tapping it opens the whole worked solution at book type size; ↺ hides the steps for a step-by-step read; ✕, Escape or a tap outside closes it.

## Special cases

- **Anchors**: q4–q6 are indented (question number at x≈208 pt) so they need `extract.anchors` regexes; q18–q23 are table rows (number in a different cell) so they need regexes too; hw1–hw14 are on scanned pages with no text layer, so they use `extract.fixedAnchors` measured from the page image ink bands.
- **Regions**: dense pages get a 2 pt band on a blank row just under the question's text (never an inverted region).
- **Folio**: `folio.json` = `{"_skip": [1]}` — the cover prints no page number; the other 10 pages carry theirs.
- No media, no graphics, no review links in this book.
